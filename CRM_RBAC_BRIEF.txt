CRM RBAC (Simple)

Roles
- Admin: full access across all organizations
- Employer: full access inside own organization
- Employee: can create and update only customers they own or are assigned to
- Investor: read-only access for organization-level visibility

Sensitive controls
- Role management: Admin + Employer
- Customer assignment: Admin + Employer
- Archive/restore customer: Admin + Employer
- Notes/contacts/addresses update: Admin/Employer/assigned Employee

Security model
- All customer records are scoped by ownerOrgId
- Important changes are logged in customer_activity for auditability
