CRM Database

Core customer data
- customers: customer master record (name, type, status, source, owner org, created by, timestamps)
- customer_profiles: extra business/personal details, lifecycle stage, tags
- customer_contacts: phone/email contact points with primary flag
- customer_addresses: billing/service addresses with primary flag
- customer_notes: timeline notes + attachment references

Control and tracking
- customer_assignments: which employee is handling which customer
- customer_activity: audit trail of create/update/status/archive/assignment actions

Search and speed
- Indexes for ownerOrgId, status, and createdAt patterns
- Full-text search on customer name and customer notes
